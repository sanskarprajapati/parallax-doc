# Title

Length: 60 s · about 135 words of narration (2.3 words a second, before pauses)

## Narration

Write it as short sentences with a turn in the middle: the accepted story first, then
"what is in question", then the reality. Spell out years and numbers the way they
should be read ("nineteen forty-four").

## Beats

One row per scene. A scene hands over to the next through something on screen, never a
plain cut or crossfade (see references/transitions.md).
